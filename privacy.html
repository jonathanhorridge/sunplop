Sunplop Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-22
Last updated: 2026-11-22

This policy explains what personal data is processed when you play Sunplop (the "App") on Android or iOS, why, and what your choices are. We wrote it to be short and plain. If anything is unclear, write to us at the address in section 1.

The short version

  • You don't create an account. We don't ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Your progress, Rays, stars, streak and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The optional daily reminder is a notification your device schedules and shows by itself. It is off until you turn it on, and no data is sent to us or anyone else for it.
  • The App shows ads from Google AdMob. Google receives device and usage data to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland), ads are only personalised if you agree, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & ads.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple or Google. We never see your card or bank details. We use RevenueCat to confirm purchases.
  • We do not sell your personal data for money. Some laws treat personalised advertising as "sharing" or "selling"; see section 9 for how to opt out.

2. What data is processed, why, and on what legal basis

2.1 Game data stored on your device

What Why Where
Level progress, stars, best times, Daily Sun results and streak, Rays balance, Jokers, owned items (for example "No ads"), settings (language, sound, music, daily reminder on/off and time), whether you have seen tutorials To run the game Only on your device (app storage). It is not sent to us.

2.2 Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and is financed by ads (banner, between-level ads, and optional reward videos you choose to watch). Ads are served by Google AdMob, provided by Google Ireland Limited (for users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland) and Google LLC (elsewhere).

When an ad is requested, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the App collects and sends to Google:

  • Device identifiers: the advertising ID (Android Advertising ID / Apple IDFA, only where available and allowed), the app set ID (Android) or identifier for vendor (iOS).
  • IP address, which can be used to estimate your approximate location (country/region/city level).
  • Ad and app interaction data: app launches, taps, ad views and video completions.
  • Diagnostics: app launch time, hang rate, energy use and crash information about the ad SDK.
  • Basic device information such as device model, operating system, language and screen size.

Purposes: showing ads, limiting how often you see the same ad, measuring ads, preventing fraud and abuse, and (only with your consent where required) personalising ads based on your interests.

Legal basis:

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a); UK GDPR; ePrivacy rules on storing and reading information on your device) for personalised ads and for storing/reading identifiers. If you do not consent, Google may still show non-personalised or limited ads, which still use some data (for example the IP address for approximate location and fraud prevention), based on our and Google's legitimate interests (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)) in financing a free app and preventing ad fraud, or as permitted under Google's "limited ads" setting.
  • Switzerland (nFADP): processing is transparent under this policy; you can object at any time (section 8).
  • iOS: we only access the IDFA if you allow it in Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt ("Allow" / "Ask App Not to Track"). You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Google acts as an independent controller for its own purposes. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. The list of Google's ad technology partners shown in the consent message may change over time.

2.3 Purchases (Apple App Store, Google Play, RevenueCat)

In-app purchases (Rays packs, the Starter offer, and "No ads") are paid only through Apple's App Store or Google Play billing. Apple or Google processes your payment as an independent controller under their own privacy policies; we never receive your card number, bank details or billing address.

To deliver and verify purchases we receive from the store, and process via RevenueCat, Inc. (USA, our service provider/processor):

  • a random, anonymous app user ID created by the App (not linked to your name or email),
  • purchase records: product, price, currency, store country, date, transaction/order ID, receipt or purchase token, and refund status,
  • basic technical data: app version, operating system, device type, locale and storefront.

The stores also give us sales reports (for accounting and tax), which can include the buyer's country and, for Google Play, in some cases the state or postal code.

Purposes: delivering what you bought, restoring non-consumable purchases, preventing fraud (fake receipts), handling refunds, customer support, accounting and tax.
Legal basis: performance of the contract with you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)); legal obligations such as bookkeeping (Art. 6(1)(c)); legitimate interest in fraud prevention (Art. 6(1)(f)).

RevenueCat privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

2.4 Consent records

Your privacy choices (for example the IAB TCF consent string and your US privacy preferences) are stored on your device by Google's User Messaging Platform so that the App can respect them. Legal basis: legal obligation to obtain and document consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(c), Art. 7(1)).

2.5 Support emails

If you email us, we process your email address, the content of your message and anything you attach (for example a screenshot or an order ID) to answer you. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering you (Art. 6(1)(f)) or performance of contract where it is about a purchase (Art. 6(1)(b)).
Our support mailbox is a Yahoo Mail account, provided to us by Yahoo International Limited (The EXO Building, North Wall Quay, Dublin 1, Ireland), which stores your emails for us. Yahoo may process data in other countries (including the US) under the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (see Yahoo's privacy policy: https://legal.yahoo.com/ie/en/yahoo/privacy/index.html).

2.6 Sharing your result

If you tap Share result, the App opens your device's share sheet or copies text (for example "Sunplop Daily ☀️ 4/12 🟨🟨…") to your clipboard. You decide where to send it. The App does not send this to us.

2.7 Daily reminder (optional notifications)

If you turn on the daily reminder, the App asks your device for permission to show notifications and then schedules local notifications on your device: at most one a day, at the time you choose, to say that a new puzzle is ready (and, for some players, how long their streak is). They are created and scheduled only on your device by the operating system's notification service. We do not use push notifications, Firebase Cloud Messaging or any other push service, there is no notification token or device ID, and no data is sent to us or to anyone else for this feature. Your choice (on/off) and the time are stored with your other game data on your device (2.1). The App never asks on first start: it asks only after you finish a daily puzzle or when you turn the reminder on yourself, and if you say no it does not keep asking. You can turn the reminder off at any time in Settings → Daily reminder, or block Sunplop's notifications in your device's settings. Legal basis: your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and your device permission.

2.8 What we do not do

We do not use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs of our own, do not collect precise (GPS) location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, do not have user accounts or chat, and do not make decisions with legal effects about you by automated means. Ad personalisation by Google is a form of profiling that only happens with your consent where the law requires it.

6. Children

Sunplop is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13 (or the higher age of digital consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.
Where Apple or Google tells the App that a player is under 13 (for example through Apple's Declared Age Range API or Google Play's Age Signals API, where the law uses these), or where a player is otherwise identified as a child, the App treats that player as a child: only non-personalised ads are requested (tagged "child-directed" and with the advertising ID not used), the tracking prompt is not shown, and purchases follow the store's parental-consent rules ("Ask to Buy" / Google Family Link). Age information received from a store is used only for these protections and is not stored longer than needed. For such players the daily reminder (2.7) is never suggested automatically and, if turned on in Settings, never mentions streaks.
If you are a parent and believe your child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

Data that leaves your device is sent encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Payment data is handled only by Apple or Google. Access to the RevenueCat dashboard and support mailbox is protected with strong passwords and two-factor authentication.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you, and get a copy (portability);
  • have it corrected or deleted;
  • restrict or object to processing, including object to processing for direct marketing and to processing based on legitimate interests;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting earlier processing: Settings → Privacy & ads → Manage privacy choices (EEA, UK, Switzerland), and on iOS also iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking;
  • complain to a supervisory authority: in Switzerland the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC, www.edoeb.admin.ch); in the EU/EEA the authority in your country; in the UK the ICO (ico.org.uk); in Brazil (LGPD) the ANPD (www.gov.br/anpd). Residents of other countries have the rights their local law gives them; contact us and we will help.

Because we don't have accounts, we usually can't link data to you. To help us find purchase records, include the store order ID (for Google Play it starts with "GPA.", for Apple it's on your Apple receipt email). We will not ask for more information than we need to verify your request. We answer within 30 days (GDPR: one month).

For data held by Google for advertising, you can also use your device settings: Android Settings → Google → Ads (reset or delete the advertising ID), iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

This section applies to residents of US states with consumer privacy laws (such as California's CCPA/CPRA, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas, Oregon and others), to the extent those laws apply to us.

Categories collected in the last 12 months: identifiers (advertising ID, app set ID/IDFV, anonymous app user ID, IP address); commercial information (in-app purchase records); internet or other electronic network activity (ad and app interactions, diagnostics); approximate geolocation (from IP address). Your rights: to know/access, delete, correct, opt out of sale/sharing/targeted advertising, and not be discriminated against for using these rights. You can use an authorised agent. If we deny your request, you can appeal by replying to our answer; if we deny the appeal, you can contact your state Attorney General. Contact: jonidge@yahoo.com.
